# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want BioBook to be a welcoming project for everyone who uses or contributes to it, regardless of age, background, gender identity and expression, disability, ethnicity, experience level, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual identity or orientation. We pledge to act in ways that keep the community open, friendly and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intentions and ask when something is unclear.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Criticise the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful for the project.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not share personal data, lab data or credentials of others.
- Help newcomers, including people who are new to programming or new to the lab.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or remarks, personal attacks, trolling or deliberate intimidation.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or email, without explicit permission.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or repeatedly pushing a request after a maintainer has declined it.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits and any other channel used for the project) and to individuals representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, hide or delete comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Maintainers will explain the reason for a moderation decision when it is appropriate to do so.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, [@Fese1000](https://github.com/Fese1000), by using GitHub's *Report content* feature or by opening a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Fese1000/BioBook/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of Conduct" if you cannot report in another way. Please do not post reports in public issues. All reports are handled confidentially, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.
